How a name gets picked

1 · Universe — actual S&P 500 members that month, cut to the 100 largest by dollar volume. Point-in-time, includes names that later delisted.
2 · Score — dip from the 52-week high (50%) + momentum (30%) + volume (20%). Needs ≥ 0.60. Kept at 0.60 because 0.65 won 2018–24 but lost out-of-sample on 2010–17.
3 · Quality gate — SEC EDGAR filings on a 90-day lag: revenue growth > 0, net margin > 0, debt/equity < 3. Equity ≤ 0 fails outright. The live screener is fail-open: only an explicit fail demotes a name to SKIP, while no data passes through on the price signal alone — deliberate, so the gate does not re-introduce survivorship bias into a universe that includes delisted names. Those rows are flagged UNVERIFIED above. (The research path in core/signals/recovery_score.py is fail-closed instead; it is not what produces these rows.)
4 · 8-K veto — a recent distress filing blocks an otherwise-valid BUY.
5 · Market gate — sleeves only go live while SPY sits ≥ 10% below its trailing 252-day high. Otherwise signals are shown but parked in the core.
6 · Size & hold — up to 10 sleeves at 10% of the satellite budget, filled the session after the signal, held 504 trading days. No stop-loss, no take-profit.
